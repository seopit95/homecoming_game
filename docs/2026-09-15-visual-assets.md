# Residential materials and first-person arm

Two original bitmap assets were made with the built-in imagegen tool and copied into this project. No external model or texture dependencies were added. Existing ghost reference assets are retained.

- `public/textures/home-surfaces-v2.png`: one four-quadrant albedo atlas for ivory wallpaper, cream ceramic tile, worn concrete and oak flooring. Used by the shared material builder throughout the building.
- `public/textures/hand-skin.png`: a neutral seamless adult hand/forearm skin albedo with shallow bump detail. Used only by the player hand material, separate from the ghost skin.

Atlas prompt:
> Create ONE square 2048x2048 photorealistic PBR ALBEDO TEXTURE ATLAS for a realistic inhabited but old 1984 Japanese apartment horror videogame. Exactly a 2x2 grid of four equal square 1024x1024 material swatches, no gaps, borders, captions, text, perspective, objects, or vignette. Pixel-perfect quadrant boundaries at the exact horizontal and vertical center. ALL FOUR squares should each tile seamlessly within themselves. Orthographic straight-on scans, uniformly flat diffuse neutral lighting, NO directional shadows or baked specular highlights. Top LEFT: warm ivory Japanese vinyl wallpaper, very fine subtle woven embossed grain, restrained natural aging and tiny scattered irregular marks, pale and fairly clean, NOT rotten, no panels or seams. Top RIGHT: small warm off-white ceramic square tiles, thin pale grey grout, natural delicate crazing and subtle tonal variation, regular tile grid. Bottom LEFT: aged light warm grey cast concrete, fine pores, small mineral flecks and subtle unevenness, realistic apartment stairwell finish, no big cracks or deep damage, no directional seams. Bottom RIGHT: lightly aged natural honey oak parquet floor, narrow parallel lengthwise wooden boards in a staggered pattern with subdued grain, fine dark join gaps and understated scuffs, realistic waxed residential wood rather than dark barn wood. Material swatches ONLY, naturally restrained contrast and physically plausible detailed photography. Used directly as a single texture atlas mapped onto 3D surfaces, so continuity and neutral albedo are critical.

Skin prompt:
> Create one square 1024x1024 seamless photorealistic albedo texture of healthy adult East Asian human hand/forearm skin for a close-up first person videogame hand model. A completely flat orthographic macro skin surface filling the entire image, no hand silhouette, no fingers or limbs, no scene, no letters. Natural light warm beige skin with restrained realistic subtle pores, fine shallow skin lines, faint irregular cool undertones and a few almost imperceptible capillaries. Fine scale realistic microstructure, even neutral diffuse lighting, no cast shadows, highlights, hard folds, wrinkles with depth, moles, wounds, blood, gore, dark spots, stylization, or plastic smoothness. Low contrast physically plausible neutral skin albedo. Seamless tiling texture edge to edge.

The arm uses a separate depth pass to preserve self-occlusion near walls. Its light follows the lens, clamps before solid surfaces and uses a short shadow near plane for nearby occluders. Aspect-aware placement supports portrait screens; animation uses simulation time to respect pause and reduced-motion settings.

The residential geometry stays procedural. These changes improve surface realism and domestic detail; they are not a photogrammetry replacement of every prop.

# Lived-in interiors design

The broad authorized goal remains a believable, polished horror game from first play to the fatal car ending. V43 fixed the capture mouth/hair integration and verified the current route; it did not finish the sparse, box-like rooms visible in that route. This pass improves the environment across three connected layers: photographed wood surfaces, constructed domestic furniture, and physical evidence of the two residents' lives. No claim of whole-game completion follows from this pass alone.

## Evidence and direction

Current source is native Sites commit `07091646a017ac7fb8d2243b4c2306083b2862a8`, with a clean checkout and 222 passing tests. V43 campaign `09-shoes.png`, `32-resident_memory.png`, `48-annex_search.png` and `53-court_search.png` expose empty wall spans, repeated box furniture and a large amount of legal but uneventful traversal. This pass addresses the two domestic rooms first while a shared wood material improves furniture in every area. It does not reinterpret the thirteen-minute known-route campaign as first-time pacing proof.

Three approaches were considered: adding scattered small props to the current boxes; replacing the entire world with imported model packs; and rebuilding the existing domestic assemblies with physical surface maps and authored room dressing. The third is selected: it changes both the silhouettes and the everyday context, retains the known interaction/navigation contract, and can be judged at real flashlight distances. Random clutter alone would preserve the demonstrated problem. A full model-pack replacement remains a possible later production decision, not an unverified promise of this implementation.

The setting is an old Korean apartment building with the restrained composition and dread of Japanese horror. Existing Japanese tenant names and notices contradict the Korean story and must become coherent Korean physical signs. This is a fictional building; `1994년 준공` supplies an ordinary older high-rise date. Do not introduce a new real-world address, victim biography, calendar chronology or brand license.

## Material layer

Use the existing `prepareVisuals` / `surface` contract and world-owned disposable texture clones. Add original 1K JPEG color, OpenGL normal and packed AO/roughness/metal maps for [Dark Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood) and [Old Wooden Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/old_wooden_floor_02), published under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Official metadata describes a 2 m furniture veneer and approximately 2.03 m floor tile. Six maps total 3,562,562 upstream bytes; verify each official MD5 and record local SHA-256, size and source URL. Bundle them in `public/textures/pbr`; gameplay must not depend on upstream network access.

Only color uses sRGB. Normal and ARM use linear decoding; normal strength must read as shallow wood grain, not eroded stone. Keep the authored tints and existing optional material parameters. Replace the old color-as-bump atlas for `wood` and `woodFloor`; do not overlay the artificial board-joint shader on photographed floor joints. Preserve plaster/concrete and character rendering. Record the six-map payload and actual render response; no generic shader framework or new dependency.

## Furniture layer

Extend the existing `props.ts` constructors rather than building a new asset registry. Add supported, human-scale sofa, drawer unit, writing/coffee table, bed and bookcase assemblies. Reuse `softBox`, `boxUV`, native Three geometry/materials and `batchStaticGeometry` inside static assemblies where needed. Existing interactive phone, radio, flashlight, evidence and doors retain identities and reachable target points.

Sofa seat height should be approximately 0.48–0.52 m; it needs a connected base, legs, rounded cushions, back and arm structure rather than a high slab. Fix its decorative pillow positions so they rest on the new cushions. Beds need visible frame/support, fitted mattress and a draped blanket with supported pillows; remove duplicate overlapping pillow meshes in the old dressing. Drawer units need a carcass, top, reveals/fronts and mounted pulls, with floor supports and no hollow exterior gaps. Bookcases need shelves and books with believable 2–5 cm thickness, separate cover/page surfaces and modest variation, replacing the identical 15 cm-thick blocks.

Reuse existing furniture footprints and anchors unless a physical correction requires a measured change. Preserve exact supporting surface heights for existing tabletop objects: home phone table 0.535 m; home study 0.80 m; home bedside torch 0.68 m; resident low table 0.45 m; resident writing desk 0.81 m; resident answering-machine cabinet 0.70 m. A visually improved assembly is rejected if it floats, exposes table props without support, leaks into nearby walls or masks an interaction. Collision bounds must reflect the resulting physical furniture rather than hiding a moved object inside an obsolete box.

## Authored room dressing and story consistency

The protagonist's large home should show avoidance and neglect: unbranded empty drink vessels, a carelessly placed jacket/keys, and an abandoned late-night living space. The victim's smaller 1704 should show a life interrupted: a clean spare work shirt, a small cared-for plant/watering item, and an ordinary appointment reminder near the existing tea/answering machine. Keep the established Han Seo-yeon night-duty role, the single shoe/envelope, `내선 17`, the missed call and refrigerator appointment. The work badge remains in the annex locker; do not duplicate that unique evidence in her home or invent family relationships.

Dressing must add recognizable forms at gameplay distance, not flat colored planes labeled as detailed props. Every floor prop has a corresponding footprint solid when it could obstruct a person; tabletop and hanging props stay inside their furniture/wall envelope. Place clothing on a visible support, paper on a table, plants on a pot/base and fixtures against a wall. Avoid coplanar faces and obvious intersections. Never cover clue text, the phone/radio controls, the flashlight, doorway, closet escape lane or the entry clues. New dressing is physical context, not a second interaction system or additional fetch objectives.

Replace Japanese notices/tenant names in `japan.ts` with coherent Korean building/safety text and a Korean-capable font. Preserve existing sign supports. Check arrows against their actual plane orientation and destination; use unambiguous text where a planar arrow would mislead. Keep the horror lighting/style, not inconsistent geography.

## Verification and acceptance

Retain an immutable V43 source and matched baseline images before editing. Geometry tests must check actual supporting ray hits, human dimensions, visible book thickness, floor/wall envelopes and reachable targets after batching, rather than fixed mesh counts. Material tests cover real loader URLs/channels, decoding, physical scale and independent texture ownership. Run a meaningful expected-red for each new invariant; preserve output that already passes rather than fabricating a baseline defect.

Inspect matched normal-game-light images at entry, sofa/phone, bedroom/torch, study/bookcase, resident entry, tea/answering machine, resident bedroom and fridge/appointment. Use actual prepared renderer with default material sides; retain wide and close views. Show normal and crouched camera heights and a complete reduced-motion pursuit/capture fixture. Reject obvious floating props, book blocks, flat cloth boards and thick furniture seams even if tests pass.

Measure main-camera submissions/triangles and frame intervals against the frozen baseline. Aim to keep changed-area draw submissions within 20 additional calls at matched viewpoints; if a meaningful assembly exceeds this, first use the existing batching/material sharing and record the tradeoff. This number is a local working performance budget, not a universal device guarantee or a substitute for good art. Final shaders must be prepared before play; whole-route logs must show whether programs grow or transitions stall.

After task and whole-branch review, freeze the exact final runtime including new files. Run the full native suite, fresh TypeScript and diff check, actual interaction/escape scenes and one final known-route campaign to the fatal ending. Record errors, raw timing, supported-prop evidence, source identity and limitations. Publish the verified source through the existing native Sites workflow/project/public audience without manual intermediate commits. Broad character art, common-area variety, first-time pacing, subjective sound and wider-device/cold-load work stay active until proven.
